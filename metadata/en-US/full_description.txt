Disastron helps you stay calm and prepared when networks fail or you need answers without leaving the device.

Features include an offline wiki (CPR essentials, evacuation, floods, and more in multiple languages), emergency numbers by detected country, dashboard with day/night and typical local conditions, SOS Morse with torch/audio/vibration, todos, and on-device AI chat via downloadable Gemma models.

Important: wiki and chat output are for education only — not medical advice. Always call local emergency services when someone is hurt. Model downloads from Hugging Face require accepting each model's license terms; weights are not bundled in the app or this repository.

Privacy: offline-first by design. Optional network use for model downloads and location when you grant OS permissions.
