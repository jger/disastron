# Content attribution

Application **source code** and most **bundled assets** in this repository are under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

Wiki and safety content follows separate attribution rules below.

## Not medical advice

Wiki articles (including CPR and first-aid reminders) and on-device chat output are for **education and convenience only**. They are **not** a substitute for certified training, professional care, or calling emergency services.

## IFRC first aid guidelines

Many wiki articles are **original short summaries** informed by the IFRC *International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025*. They are not verbatim copies of the IFRC document.

IFRC permits non-commercial copying and adaptation with source acknowledgment. See the IFRC guidelines PDF:

https://www.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/IFRC%20International%20First%20Aid%2C%20Resuscitation%20and%20Education%20Guidelines%202025.pdf

Each wiki article includes a **Sources** section with the relevant IFRC link where applicable.

Disastron is a **free, open-source app** distributed without charge. Maintainers treat Play Store and F-Droid distribution as non-commercial community access, not paid reproduction of IFRC materials.

## American Red Cross references

Some wiki articles link to American Red Cross public web pages (for example communication-plan guidance). Disastron does **not** copy ARC page text or images into the repository; links are provided for reader reference only.
