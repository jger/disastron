title: Trip & home preparedness
summary: Before you travel or shelter in place.
---
## Home kit basics
- **Water** (3-day supply per person when feasible)
- **Food** that needs no cooking or minimal fuel
- **Flashlights** + spare batteries / power bank
- **First aid** kit + needed prescriptions
- **Warm layers**, blankets, sturdy gloves
- **Documents** copies (ID, insurance) in waterproof pouch
- **Cash** small denominations

## Before a trip
- Share itinerary + check-in schedule with one trusted contact
- Download offline maps for the route
- Pack a paper list of emergency numbers for your destinations
- Move ICE/medical info to lock screen when supported
