---
title: General safety mindset
summary: Stay calm, assess, act, reassess.
---
## Priorities
1. **Protect life** — yourself first when possible, then others.
2. **Reduce danger** — fire, traffic, electricity, structures.
3. **Call for help** when networks allow — give location and nature of emergency.
4. **Document lightly** — photos/notes only when safe.

## If alone
- Pause 10 seconds to scan for ongoing hazards.
- Identify **two exits** if indoors.
- Pack essentials only if evacuation is ordered.
