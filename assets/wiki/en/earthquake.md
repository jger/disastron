---
title: Earthquake (initial shake)
summary: Drop, Cover, Hold — then assess.
---
## During shaking
- **Drop, Cover, Hold On** under sturdy furniture or beside interior wall; avoid windows/glass.
- If outdoors: move away from buildings/facades/power lines.

## After shaking
- Expect aftershocks; check gas only if trained; prepare to evacuate if structure is unsafe.
