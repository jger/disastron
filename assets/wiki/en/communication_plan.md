title: Communication plan
summary: When networks fail, use agreed rally points.
---
## Offline tips
- Agree an **out-of-area contact** everyone checks in with.
- Text often works when voice fails; short messages conserve battery.
- Conserve power: airplane mode except scheduled checks.

Keep ICE (**In Case of Emergency**) contacts on lock screen when supported.
