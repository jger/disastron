---
title: Heavy bleeding
summary: Pressure, elevation, tourniquet as last resort.
---
## Steps
1. **Direct pressure** with clean cloth; keep continuous pressure.
2. **Elevate** limb if no fracture suspected.
3. **Tourniquet** only for life-threatening limb bleeding that cannot be controlled — note time applied; loosen only by trained help.

Seek professional care immediately after stabilizing.
