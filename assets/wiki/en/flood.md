---
title: Flooding
summary: Never drive through flood water.
---
## Rules
- **Turn around, don’t drown** — ~30 cm moving water can sweep a vehicle.
- Avoid wading — debris, sewage, electricity hazards.
- Move vertically if flash flood risk; avoid basements.
