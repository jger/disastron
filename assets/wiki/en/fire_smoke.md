---
title: Fire & smoke indoors
summary: Stay low, feel doors, don’t waste air.
---
## If smoke
- **Stay low** — cooler, clearer air near floor.
- **Test doors** with back of hand; hot door = find another route.
- Close doors behind you to slow spread.

## If trapped
- Seal door gaps with cloth; signal from window; minimize shouting to conserve air.
