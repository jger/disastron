## Before you move
- Grab **ID, meds, phone/charger**, minimal water.
- Wear sturdy shoes; carry a light.

## During evacuation
- Prefer official routes; avoid elevators if fire/smoke suspected.
- Assist children/elderly only when it does not put you at extreme risk.
- Meet at the agreed **muster point** if one exists.
